About

I’m Matt Simmons. I test AI claims before believing them. In December 2025 I built a benchmark that put seven language models through JSON extraction, mathematical reasoning, and executive summarization against fixed thresholds. One passed, and Towards AI published the results.

Behind that sits a decade of teaching people to use complicated tools. Seven years as a graduate instructor at UC Santa Cruz, twenty-plus courses, more than a thousand students across every level of technical comfort. Earlier, I managed twenty-plus institutional accounts for a B2B education platform, including Harvard and MIT, with a 95 percent renewal rate. I hold a Ph.D. from UC Santa Cruz, which in practice means I read what a vendor is actually selling rather than what the deck appears to say.

I’m based in San Diego and deliberately work with a small number of clients at a time.
