About Matt Simmons

I test AI claims before believing them. In December 2025 I built a benchmark that put seven commercial language models through JSON extraction, mathematical reasoning, and executive summarization against fixed thresholds. One passed, and Towards AI published the framework and results. That is the habit I bring to every engagement: measure first, then advise.

Behind that sits a decade of teaching people to use complicated tools. Seven years as a graduate instructor at UC Santa Cruz — twenty-plus courses, more than a thousand students across every level of technical comfort. More recently, I onboarded and trained over two hundred distributed professionals for an AI evaluation firm, cutting performance variance across the group by 40 percent.

Earlier, I managed twenty-plus institutional accounts for a B2B education platform, including Harvard and MIT, with a 95 percent renewal rate. I hold a Ph.D. from UC Santa Cruz, which in practice means I read what a vendor is actually selling rather than what the deck appears to say.

How the practice runs

Diagnosis is free; treatment is billed. The first 30-minute fit call costs nothing. Every engagement after it is scoped in writing, with a fixed price, before any billable work begins.

Independent means independent. I take no vendor commissions, referral fees, or reseller margins. Advice is priced so that the only person paying me is you.

Few clients, on purpose. I work with a small number of owner-led businesses at a time, so every project gets my direct involvement rather than a process.
