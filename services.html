Services & Rate Card

Every engagement is scoped in writing before it starts: fixed deliverables, a fixed fee, and no vendor commissions on any of it.

Advisory

Assessment $2,800 – $3,200 fixed · 3–4 weeks

Focused AI Assessment

A structured look at one function of your business — the one where the pain is — that ranks where AI would actually pay off, what it would cost, and what to skip. One function done properly beats all of them done thinly, and the report is yours whether or not I do the work that follows.

  • One function or process, chosen together on the fit call
  • Up to four structured interviews with the people who actually do the work
  • A written report, six to eight pages, ranking each opportunity by payoff, cost, and effort
  • A readout call, and numbers that hold up on their own
Adoption $2,250 – $3,000 fixed · 3–4 weeks

AI Adoption Sprint

Your team already pays for AI tools. This gets them used. Hands-on training built from a decade of teaching people to work with complicated systems — a thousand-plus students at UC Santa Cruz, two hundred more at an AI evaluation firm — with materials your team keeps.

  • Use-case mapping by role, so the training is about your work rather than the tools
  • Working sessions with the team, not a slide deck
  • A playbook your team keeps: what to use for what, prompts, and guardrails
  • A follow-up structure that does not depend on me staying
Diligence $1,200 fixed · 1 week

Vendor Second Opinion

Before you sign an AI contract or renewal, an independent read on the pitch already on your table: what is actually being sold, what it really costs to run, and whether there is a cheaper way to get the same result. One vendor, one decision, delivered in writing.

  • What is actually being sold — and what the deck is really claiming
  • The true run-rate cost: seats, usage, implementation, and what it takes to leave
  • The alternative, costed: a cheaper tool or an in-house build
  • A written memo with a recommendation and the reasoning shown

On independence: I take no vendor commissions or referral fees. I’ll also be straight about the other side of that coin — if the answer is “build it instead,” that could be work I’d do, so the memo prices the build and names other people who could do it. And when the answer is “buy it, you don’t need me,” that is what it says.

AI Visibility
